# Community Photos - Instructions

## Please add the following photos to the `media/images/` folder:

### 1. **sukkot-building.jpg**
- Photo of students building sukkot for elderly people
- Shows students in action helping the community
- Recommended size: 800x600px or larger

### 2. **memorial-visits.jpg**
- Photo of students visiting families of fallen soldiers
- Can show students with cakes or during a visit
- Should convey respect and compassion
- Recommended size: 800x600px or larger

### 3. **agriculture-volunteering.jpg**
- Photo of students doing agricultural work
- Can show harvesting, planting, or farm work
- Should show connection to the land
- Recommended size: 800x600px or larger

## Important Notes:
- All images should be in JPG format
- Minimum resolution: 800x600 pixels
- Ideal resolution: 1200x900 pixels or higher
- Make sure images are well-lit and clear
- Images should show students actively engaged in the activities

## Current Status:
The website is set up to display these images. Once you add them to the `media/images/` folder, they will automatically appear in the Community Involvement section.
